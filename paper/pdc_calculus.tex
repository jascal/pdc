\begin{abstract}
Projective Derivation Calculus (PDC) extends the decode stratum of Projective
Incidence Calculus (PIC) with position-indexed reads, alternative bodies of
an atom, explicit Viterbi and marginal readouts, and an external theory
judgment. Its one-position, one-body vocabulary fragment is kernel-checked
against PIC. Finite artifacts retain the body table, readout, margin,
fragility, provenance and covered candidate set. Frozen GPT-2 measurements
identify the read mixture on 108 head/query instances and exhibit readout
fragility on competing copy routes. A fixed-computation admission oracle
admits a positive control, refuses a rational three-context control, and
finds no singleton irreducibility certificates on 80 Qwen positions.
An independently checked addition experiment then triggers the program's
preregistered epistemic stop rule: among certified commitments, 7/8 Viterbi
answers and 9/10 marginal answers are false in the finite theory.
The margin certificate preserves a covered score decision; that property
does not establish truth on this domain. Admission learning is negative,
and the final held-out service audit has zero coverage. Admitted-memory
transfer and useful service remain unestablished. This paper
records the stopped stage and does not claim the full program is complete.
\end{abstract}

\section{Objects and the PIC seam}

\status{definition} A source contributes an incidence to an atom. A body is
a finite source coalition; its bracket adds those incidences. For a biased
decoder, the executable complete-body bracket includes the atom bias once.
Thus a body's annotation is temperature independent. Alternatives of an
atom are combined by the temperature family
\[
 \alpha_T(a)=T\log\sum_{P\in\deriv(a)}
      \exp\bigl(\bracket{P}{a}/T\bigr),\qquad T>0.
\]
The two readouts are
\[
 \Vit(a)=\max_{P\in\deriv(a)}\bracket{P}{a},\qquad
 \Mar_T(a)=\alpha_T(a).
\]
An empty body set has negative-infinity score and cannot win. Winner sets
retain ties; a tied readout does not commit an atom. Body identifiers retain
multiplicity when distinct bodies have equal brackets. These sums can count
shared subbodies repeatedly. No possible-world or disjoint-explanation
probability semantics is asserted.

\status{proved} Restrict the chart to one position, vocabulary atoms, at most
one body per atom, and no external theory. For the singleton full-source
coalition the annotation is PIC's biased logit
\[
 \alpha_T(v)=\bracket{S}{v}=L(v)
       =\left\langle\sum_{j\in S}d_j,U_v\right\rangle+b_v.
\]
In the Isabelle representation, a present score is \path{Some} of this
real value; \path{None} represents an absent atom. The equality to a finite
logit is the displayed singleton case. Empty atoms are not assigned a
finite logit by this translation. Positive-temperature softmax preserves
logit order and argmax sets. Empty/singleton body tables have equal Viterbi
and marginal annotations, so their winner sets are temperature independent.

These statements import the actual PIC definitions, rather than a parallel
logit semantics. Their artifacts are \path{i-orca/examples/pdc/PDC_Derive.thy}:
\path{mar_singleton}, \path{one_body_agreement},
\path{one_body_temperature_invariance}, and the four
\path{pic.specialization_alpha_eq_logit},
\path{pic.specialization_alpha_eq_residual},
\path{pic.specialization_softmax_order}, and
\path{pic.specialization_softmax_argmax} lemmas. The strict session is \path{PDC},
parent \path{PIC_Core}; \path{quick_and_dirty=false}, zero proof holes.

\status{witness, proved} Let atom A have two distinct bodies of score zero
and atom B have one body of score $1/2$. At $T=1$,
\[
 \Vit(A)=0<1/2=\Vit(B),\qquad
 \Mar_1(A)=\log 2>1/2=\Mar_1(B).
\]
The kernel lemmas \path{witness_viterbi}, \path{witness_marginal} and
\path{witness_fragile} check both winners and their disagreement.
The one-body inheritance stops when alternatives are summed inside an atom.

\section{Reads, bodies and finite exports}

\status{definition} A head reads key positions through incidences
$i\mathbin{\triangleright_h}j=\langle q_i,k_j\rangle$. Under a scaled-query
convention the native GPT-2 score is this incidence divided by
$\sqrt{d_h}$. The head write is its softmax-weighted value mixture. Its output
projection is a further linear write; the shared projection bias belongs
to the block. Value mixtures, vocabulary decisions and external truth are
distinct objects in the artifact.

\section{Admission and its limits}

\status{definition} Crystallization admits an atom as a source only under
a margin rule. A common score offset does not affect a decision. If the
coalition margin exceeds $\gamma>2\delta$ and the admitted source's score
errors, after a common offset, are bounded by $\delta$ at the target and
every covered rival, its decision survives. The omitted-coalition form
applies the same premise to the tail incidences. The candidate set must
remain unchanged throughout the certificate.

\status{proved} \path{i-orca/examples/pdc/PDC_Admission.thy} packages PIC's
pointwise margin certificate as \path{admitted_source_preserves_decision},
\path{pic.crystallization_admitted_write}, and
\path{pic.crystallization_complement_certified}. A fourth lemma,
\path{pairwise_source_preserves_decision}, permits a guard per rival.
For a target t and rival v, the body gap must exceed the absolute drift of
that pair's gap from coalition scores to the source scores. Each pair can
use its own offset and $\delta$; no whole-vocabulary perturbation ball is
introduced. All four lemmas pass the strict Isabelle session. Their
conclusions are score decisions, not truth judgments.

\paragraph{Scope of all kernel claims.}
The kernel results concern finite score identities, readout comparisons and
preservation of covered score decisions under explicit premises. They do
not establish external-theory truth, useful reasoning, or agreement with
an unrestricted full-vocabulary host outside the covered candidate set.
A nonzero admission rate alone would establish none of those stronger
claims. Full-host agreement and external-theory judgments require their
own independently checked evidence; protected-bank preservation is not
generalization. R7's fixed-budget negative does not identify a unique
bottleneck or prove that admission is unlearnable.


\status{empirical, R4} The oracle fixes measured source writes and frees
one shared affine decoder frame. Faithfulness and a tested singleton's
decision are linear inequalities. Feasible frames and solver statuses are
retained; infeasibility requires checked Farkas multipliers. Solver errors
remain unknown. Admission is checked separately from LP reducibility.

\section{External truth: a preregistered failure}

\status{definition} $P\vdash a$ records a source-coalition score decision.
$\mathcal T\models a$ is a separate decidable judgment. A recorded
commitment can be affirmed by both, or be a hallucination relative to the
theory. A miss occurs when the theory affirms an atom that no recorded body
strictly decides. A fragile truth is selected marginally but not by Viterbi.
None of these judgments is recovered merely by renaming a host margin.

\status{empirical, R5} The fixed theory is ordinary integer addition for
operands 0--4 and answers 0--8. Three fixed prompt templates cover all 25
ordered pairs, giving 75 evaluation contexts. A fourth template's 25
contexts were reserved until the final audit below. Leading-space answer tokens
are fixed before any forward call. Each atom has three fixed bodies: the
full source coalition, the attention-block coalition, and the MLP-block
coalition. The 2,025 annotations are retained in the exported bundle.

The advance threshold is $\gamma=0.5$. Both the named readout and its chosen
best-bracket body's margin must clear this threshold. Each covered rival
must also pass the pairwise drift guard. Ties, coverage mismatches and
failed guards abstain. The theory does not enter that selection. The
hallucination bar is strictly below 10\%; zero commitments is untested.
The preregistered wide-miss threshold is 25\%.

There are 47 misses, three fragile contexts, and three fragile truths.
Both readouts miss the advance bar widely. Both finite-domain epistemic
claims are retired; the R5 stop restriction is active.

\status{empirical} Native and independent NumPy final-position predictions
agree 75/75; the observer's hidden-state effect is zero. Raw source-sum,
final-LN-folding and full-body candidate-score maximum errors are,
respectively, $1.682\cdot10^{-5}$, $1.702\cdot10^{-4}$ and
$2.399\cdot10^{-4}$. The finite pairwise check uses actual native candidate
scores, so those discrepancies are not hidden behind an assumed global
epsilon. A separate auditor reconstructs every source/body annotation,
both readouts and every commit/abstain decision. Maximum annotation
discrepancy is zero. A separate theory checker computes addition from the
operands and rejects a purported commitment lacking a body decision.

One retained case makes the limitation concrete: \path{t2-a0-b0} asks for
$0+0$ under the ordinary-addition prompt. Both readouts commit 1 using the
MLP coalition; the unrestricted native host also predicts 1. The body
margin is 3.040224 and minimum pairwise guard slack is 1.040681. The
certificate holds while the theory rejects the answer. An attention body
in the same chart decides 0, so this case is not a miss. The negative
result concerns this frozen model, domain, prompts, candidate set, bodies
and threshold. It is not an impossibility theorem about all models.

The full evidence is \path{fieldrun/experiments/pdc/R5_PREREG.md},

\path{r5_theory_results.json}. Their input hashes are retained. The
certificate covers nine tokens; it does not certify the unrestricted
vocabulary argmax, whose agreement is separately reported above.

\section{The stopped program and remaining obligations}

\status{empirical, R6} A fixed four-arm comparison now measures all 27
triples in $\{0,1,2\}^3$ for $(a+b)+c$. It reuses nine first-hop R5 captures
and freezes exact token assembly, memory vectors and admission attempts
before any new hop-2 output. All nine first hops are refused by the recorded
native-frame singleton procedure, giving 27 refused triples and no admitted
stratum. Re-derive and crystallize each score 3/27; generated append and tool
append each score 9/27. Generated append retains all three baseline-correct
cases and adds six, a 22.22-point gain above the advance five-point bar.
This supports the operational comparison on this refused slice. Admitted
redundancy and actual admitted replay remain untested. The tool and generated
arms have equal aggregate accuracy but different correctness on four cases;
no pointwise tool upper bound or semantic-content necessity is proved.

Independent NumPy final-position predictions agree 108/108. Every refused
crystallize case is exactly identical to re-derive and injects no source.
A separate zero/nonzero replay engineering check finds zero-write identity,
a nonzero hidden effect of 0.537842 and agreement of the induced change with
NumPy within $6.390\cdot10^{-5}$. The baseline's absolute hidden mismatch
of 0.003174 is separately retained; it is not claimed below the initially
attempted $10^{-3}$ absolute diagnostic. These engineering probes are not
admitted memory observations. See
\path{fieldrun/experiments/pdc/R6_PREREG.md}, \path{R6_RESULTS.md}, and
\path{r6_results.json}. The exported two-hop graph has 117 independently
checked affine decode nodes and 108 provenance paths, with 27 generated,
27 external-theory tool and 54 no-memory edges. Its finite margin coverage
is distinct from its observed unrestricted native token.

\status{empirical, R7 negative} Two preregistered 200-proposal arms
learn an untied output frame and bias with fixed native writes and gates.
Both decode-only and admission-objective arms retain zero admissions out
of 75; the improvement and control-advantage gates fail. All 183 protected
full-host predictions remain unchanged. All accepted steps have nonzero
motion, satisfy logged per-step drift premises, and retain $\mathrm{FP}\ge W$.
Independent NumPy endpoint reconstruction agrees within $5.41\cdot10^{-13}$.
These are protected-bank observations, not a generalization result; 25
reserved contexts remained untouched until the final audit below. See
\path{pil/docs/notes/pdc_r7_prereg.md}, \path{pdc_r7_outcome.md}, and
\path{pil/results/pdc_r7/verification.json}. R6's admitted-side claim remains
unmeasured, R5 remains retired, and no chart-depth versus layer-depth
function is claimed.

\status{empirical, saved-frame diagnostic} A separately frozen offline
four-arm analysis compares original singleton admission, a halved absolute
margin floor, highest-source plus bias, and random-source plus bias.
All four admit zero of 75 at initialization and both R7 endpoints; all
183 protected outputs remain unchanged. At each endpoint, nine contexts
have a strict singleton decision matching the covered host target without
the stronger certificate, but no singleton passes $m>2\delta$. Thus the
absolute floor alone cannot explain all refusals. This identifies no unique
causal learning bottleneck and examines no larger coalition. Conditional
host agreement among admissions is undefined. No model call or final
held-out input is used. See
\path{pil/docs/notes/pdc_r7_diagnostic_prereg.md},
\path{pdc_r7_diagnostic_outcome.md}, and
\path{pil/results/pdc_r7_diagnostic/verification.json}.
The original negative R7 gate and v0.10 archive remain unchanged.

\status{empirical, R8-H} A preregistered host-only continuation now runs a
fresh native subset forward and returns a served bundle or an explicit
abstention. A tokenizer-text rule freezes 1,703 probe tokens before their
scores are inspected. Its certificate checks the chosen body's target
against every probe, while the derive query retains the nine answer atoms.
The independent audit checks 383,175 body/probe annotations and finds zero
source-write and probe-score discrepancy against a full baseline. Native
output-row dot calls fall from 3,769,275 to 127,725 across the 75 contexts,
with 383,175 additional body/probe dots in each arm. No layer or position
is skipped. Both readouts abstain on all 75 contexts. The required nonempty
coverage gate therefore fails; host agreement and hallucination rates among
commitments are undefined. This compute reduction produces no usable
answers under the fixed policy. See
\path{fieldrun/experiments/pdc/R8H_PREREG.md}, its timing addendum,
\path{R8H_RESULTS.md}, and \path{r8h_results.json}.

One fixed ordered timing pair gives 13.355 seconds end to end for the full
baseline and 12.871 for subset service; these descriptive timings do not
establish a general speedup. The runtime also serves a single named
non-held-out request and rejects a reserved request before execution.
The final audit below subsequently consumed the 25 reserved contexts. R8's original useful
truth/host service gate is \status{open}; theory soundness is not advertised
from the failed margin instrument.

outcomes, a one-shot preregistered audit opens the 25 reserved template-3
contexts with the unchanged original R8-H policy. Both readouts abstain
on all 25. Host agreement and hallucination among commitments are undefined;

paper documents a stopped stage rather than achievement of the north star.
